Storm Risks and Weather Patterns

Storms often happen during the rainy season, especially when there is heavy rain, strong wind, and thunderstorms.

Storms form when warm, moist air rises and meets cooler air, creating clouds, rain, and strong winds.

Dark clouds, strong winds, thunder, lightning, and heavy rain can be signs of an approaching storm.

Storms can damage homes, flood roads, destroy crops, and cause power cuts.
Storm Preparedness and Risk Reduction

People can prepare by checking weather forecasts, preparing emergency supplies, and securing their homes.

People should stay indoors, avoid flooded areas, and keep away from trees and power lines.

We can clear drains, secure roofs, plant trees, and improve drainage systems.

Communities can share storm warnings through radio, social media, and local announcements.
