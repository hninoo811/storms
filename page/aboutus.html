WHO WE ARE

Our Purpose

Discover our mission, vision, and goals for building safer and more prepared communities.

01 / MISSION

Our Mission

To help local communities understand severe storm risks and learn effective ways to prepare, stay safe, and minimize potential damage.

02 / VISION

Our Vision

To create a well-informed and resilient community where people are prepared for severe storms and can protect lives and property.

03 / GOALS

Our Goals

  • Provide clear and easy-to-understand storm preparedness information.
  • Help people recognize storm risks and understand safety precautions.
  • Promote actions that can reduce storm-related damage and protect lives.
